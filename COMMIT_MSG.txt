Drop one-off exploration scripts before the repo goes public

Six probes that answered a question once and then stopped earning their
place: whether the main repo was gated, whether the evaluation mirrors
carried a factory label, how large the shards were. Their answers live in the
README and the commit log, and the scripts stay recoverable from history.

A src/ directory whose first impression is five dead probe files argues
against the work inside it, and this repo's entire job is to be credible on
sight.

Kept verify_access.py (anyone using --source gated will hit a 401 and need
it), check_gate.py (shows the saturation gate separating real footage from
broken), and smoke.py (end-to-end plumbing against the live source).
